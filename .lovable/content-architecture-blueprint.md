# 🏗️ LocalDominate.org – Content Architecture Blueprint
## Hierarchical Local SEO Knowledge Structure

> **Version:** 1.0 | **Date:** 2026-03-05  
> **Goal:** Topical authority for Local SEO in DACH (DE/AT/CH)  
> **Total Planned Pages:** 310 (10 Pillars + 40 Hubs + 100 Industries + 80 Cities + 60 Troubleshooting + 20 Tools)

---

## 📐 Architecture Overview

```
Pillar Pages (10)
├── Hub Pages (40) — 4 per pillar avg
│   ├── Industry Pages (100) — cluster around hubs
│   ├── City Pages (80) — geo-targeted clusters
│   ├── Troubleshooting (60) — problem-solution pairs
│   └── Tools/Templates (20) — interactive utilities
└── SEO Lexikon (55+ terms) — cross-linked definitions
```

- All pages include `data-ai-summary` attributes on key paragraphs
- `data-ai-extractable` on factual claims, statistics, steps
- `llms-full.txt` updated with each new page batch
