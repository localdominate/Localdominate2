Questions brands ask

Is the content marked as a collaboration?

Yes, always. Every sponsored post carries the paid partnership label of the platform, and the caption starts with the word “Ad”.

Can we reuse the content?

Yes. Every rate includes organic use on your own website and social channels for six months. Use in paid ads costs 30% extra and is agreed in the brief.

Can we review before it goes live?

Yes. You see Reels, posts and captions before they go live, and two rounds of changes are included. Stories from the day go out live and follow the agreed talking points.

What results do we get back?

Seven days after the last post you get a one-page report with views, reach, saves, shares and link taps, as screenshots from the platform insights.

Which languages does Noa work in?

English and French. Captions can carry a German or Italian translation if you supply it.

Who pays for travel, stays and products?

The host covers the stay, flights and transfers for Noa and one assistant. Products should arrive at least two weeks before the shoot.

Is a small brand a fit?

Yes, if the place or product fits the feed. The smallest booking is one story set, and independent hotels are welcome.
